# WORLD — Master diary

This is the persistent creative log for the world. Append dated entries; do not rewrite history.

## 2026-11-13 — The first inhabitants begin to live
The world moves beyond static mannequin shapes. The player and inhabitants receive articulated shoulder, hip, arm and leg pivots. Walking drives alternating arm and leg swings; idle motion adds small breathing/head movements. The player silhouette gains layered clothing, cuffs, boots, hair fringe and eyes.

Creative direction:
- WORLD must not look like Minecraft. Avoid the cubic, block-stacked look.
- Use a soft low-poly language with clean silhouettes, carefully shaped forms, smoother terrain transitions and more precise polygons.
- People should look like stylised inhabitants, not wooden mannequins.
- A city must feel like a real place: coherent streets, building plots, landmarks, public spaces, street furniture, vegetation, services and people.
- Every settlement should reflect its region's climate, culture, materials and purpose.
- The first city is the founding settlement; its layout and identity will be established before expanding to other regions.

World scale decision:
- Target area is 300 km², represented as 20 km × 15 km.
- World generation and rendering must be streamed in chunks. Never create the entire 300 km² as one scene.
- The first city starts in the south-west corner and becomes the visual and narrative anchor of the world.

Master's promise:
Build with continuity. A named place, character, road or landmark that enters the world should remain part of its history. Keep a record of important changes and preserve player discoveries.
